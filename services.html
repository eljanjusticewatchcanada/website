Services

Four service lines

Each engagement ends with a concrete deliverable and clear responsibilities for follow-up.

Corporate compliance support

We organize corporate and governance records, map existing processes, prepare compliance calendars, identify missing documents and track corrective work.
Deliverables: records inventory, process checklist and action register.

Factual due diligence

We review source documents and authorized public records, record discrepancies and distinguish verified facts from assertions.
Deliverables: evidence index, factual chronology and an issues register with verification priorities.

Community readiness and referral

We support nonprofit and grant readiness with program evidence, budgets supplied by management and governance records. We develop general rights-awareness materials and coordinate referrals to independently retained professionals.

Humanitarian access to justice

We develop community referral pathways, organize authorized case records and publish general rights education. Independent Nigerian counsel determines legal merit and accepts or declines any retainer.

An example. For charity readiness, Eljan can map programs to supporting records, flag missing governance documents and coordinate an accountant or charity lawyer. Counsel assesses eligibility and reviews governing-document changes.
