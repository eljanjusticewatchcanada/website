What Eljan is, and is notEljan is a consulting company. It does not act as a Canadian law firm, issue Canadian legal opinions or provide immigration representation.

What we do

Eljan helps organizations turn scattered records and unclear processes into reliable files, documented decisions and practical action plans.

Corporate compliance support

Organized corporate and governance records, mapped processes and tracked corrective work.

Factual due diligence

Source documents reviewed, discrepancies recorded, verified facts kept apart from assertions.

Community readiness and referral

Support for nonprofit and grant readiness, and referrals to independent professionals.

Access to justice

General rights education, responsible documentation and referral coordination.

Who we serve

Small and mid-sized businesses, newcomer-led enterprises, nonprofits, community organizations and cross-border project teams that need stronger records, clearer controls or a well-prepared handoff to specialist advisers.

We separate verified facts from client statements and unresolved questions, and every finding can be traced to the records reviewed.
