How We Work

From intake to action

A consistent review process makes it easier for management and independent advisers to rely on the underlying record.

Scope and accept

We identify the client, objective, deadline, conflicts, fees and exclusions, and agree how sensitive documents will be transferred.

Collect and verify

We record source, date and provenance, separate verified facts from reported information and gaps, and limit collection to what the engagement needs.

Report and route

We set out observations, limitations, priorities and next steps. Questions of legal interpretation, liability, regulated filings or tax treatment go to the appropriate adviser.

Implement and close

We track agreed actions, owners and due dates, record client decisions and close the file under the agreed retention policy.

Fees are fixed for defined reviews and readiness packages, with retainers for ongoing documentation support and agreed fees for workshops or projects.

Corporate compliance support, factual due diligence and access-to-justice initiatives. Clear facts. Responsible action.
