Access to Justice

Education, documentation and referral

Eljan supports people to understand their rights and reach independent professionals.

What we do

We build referral relationships with community groups and independent lawyers, use consent-based intake, and assess urgency, documentation needs and safeguarding. Independent counsel evaluates the merits of any matter and decides whether to take a case.

From Canada, Eljan manages screening, factual chronologies, evidence indexes and partner reporting. Counsel controls legal strategy and gives advice directly under a separate engagement.

We publish general rights education and run workshops. Identifiable or live-case material is only shared with informed consent and counsel clearance.

Please note

This website does not replace secure intake or professional review, and nothing on it is legal advice. Please do not send case details by email or through this site.
