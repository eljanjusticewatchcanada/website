About

A Canadian consulting company

Eljan Justicewatch Canada Inc. is federally incorporated and based in Surrey, British Columbia.

Who we are

Eljan is a Canadian consulting brand led by Kester Osifo Elebesunu, a Nigerian-trained corporate lawyer. The company helps organizations build reliable records and make well-documented decisions, and it supports access-to-justice work through public education, responsible documentation and referral coordination.

Eljan Justicewatch Canada Inc. was incorporated under the Canada Business Corporations Act. Corporation No. 1831372-5.

What we are not

Eljan does not act as a Canadian law firm, does not issue Canadian legal opinions and does not provide immigration representation. Questions of legal interpretation, liability, regulated filings or tax treatment are referred to the appropriate independent adviser, who owns that advice.

How we think about trust

Public claims should reflect records, completed work and permissions that we can produce. We report only evidenced results and we do not guarantee outcomes.

Corporate compliance support, factual due diligence and access-to-justice initiatives. Clear facts. Responsible action.
